# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
- Affiliate network operators, performance marketing managers, and campaign administrators managing multi-network offer portfolios.
- Publishers, media buyers, and affiliate partners tracking outbound referral traffic, conversion postbacks, and revenue share.

## Product Purpose
Oval Media Affiliate Platform is an enterprise-grade performance marketing and attribution platform. It enables operators to configure multi-tier campaigns, manage advertiser networks and custom tracking domains, route clicks with sub-millisecond redirect speeds, and record server-to-server (S2S) conversion postbacks with complete financial idempotency.

## Positioning
A Zero-Leak attribution engine combined with a modern 3D tactile operations console that guarantees zero revenue or traffic loss even when partner tracking parameters are missing or malformed.

## Operating Context
Performance ad-tech workflows requiring high-concurrency click redirection, real-time postback webhook delivery, granular geo/device targeting rules, and scannable tabular analytics.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- High-throughput click redirection with macro parameter tokenization (`{clickid}`, `{aff_id}`, `{offer_id}`, `{sub1}-{sub5}`, etc.).
- Multi-tier targeting rules across countries, device categories, operating systems, and browsers.
- S2S postback webhooks with SSRF protection and advertiser security token verification.
- Safe master account fallback for missing/unregistered affiliate parameters.
- Multi-theme Dark Mode and Light Mode with high-contrast typography.
- Table-first parameter inspection and 1-click token copying.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Oval Media Affiliate Tracking
- Theme Style: Modern 3D gradient depth, subtle top-edge bevel highlights, and high-contrast typography.
- Copy Tone: Plain English suitable for international teams; zero obscure developer jargon.

## Product Principles
1. Zero-Leak Traffic Integrity: Never drop referral traffic; safely fallback to master account when parameters are missing.
2. Table-First Visual Scannability: Dense parameters, webhooks, and rules must be displayed in clean tables, never wrapping badge clouds.
3. 1-Click Tactile Interactivity: Macro tokens and URLs must be copyable or appendable in a single click with instant feedback.
4. High Contrast & Dual Theme: Flawless contrast across Dark and Light modes with clear typographic hierarchy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- High contrast WCAG AA compliant text color ratios (`--color-fg`, `--color-fg-heading`).
- Focus rings, keyboard tab navigation, and active scale animations on all interactive controls.
- Status badges pair color indicators with explicit text labels and icons for color-blind accessibility.
