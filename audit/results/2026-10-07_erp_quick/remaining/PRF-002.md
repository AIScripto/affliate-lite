# PRF-002: Report endpoints compute everything on every read, with no pagination or cache

**Severity:** Medium  
**Status:** Closed as measured, no code change

## Why it was left
Measured on 2,029 employees and 68,947 assessments: every report answers in 0.35 to 0.42 s, except YoY growth at 1.47 s. 10 parallel readiness reports finish in 1.5 s and the server stays responsive. The audit's suspected `.some()` scan is already a Set; the real cost is loading the rows and the scoring step, which must not be touched. A cache would save only milliseconds and risks stale scores. Details: `prf-002-measurement.md`.

## Risk of leaving it
None at today's size. Two responses are large: gap matrix 8.2 MB and competency matrix 4.4 MB (nginx compresses them).

## When to revisit
Headcount near 3,000, or YoY growth slower than 3 s, or real multi-year history (the test had only one period with data).

## What to do then
Profile first. Then page or cap the two large matrices. Do not cache scores.
