# UXD-002: The whole app is one URL; no deep links or Back button

**Severity:** Medium  
**Status:** Waiting for your decision (D5)

## Why it was left
A real URL for each screen touches almost every screen and the router. It should come after the other quality work, and together with the router upgrade.

## Risk of leaving it
Convenience only: people cannot bookmark or share a screen, and the browser Back button leaves the app.

## When to revisit
When people ask for shareable links.

## What to do then
One route per tab and per Setup section, return to the page after login, tests for every route and its access rule.

## Needed from you
D5: yes or no.
