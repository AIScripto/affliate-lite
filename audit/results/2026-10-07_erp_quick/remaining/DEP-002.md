# DEP-002: Frontend production dependencies have known vulnerabilities

**Severity:** Medium  
**Status:** Done, except two parts left on purpose

## Why it was left
Done: axios is 1.20 (the fixed version, minimum raised), and Tailwind moved to `devDependencies` because it only builds CSS (production audit went from 9 findings to 2). Left: (1) react-router v7. The fix exists only in v7, a major migration. Both advisories do not apply here: one is an open redirect through user-controlled links (every navigation in the app uses a fixed path such as /login or /dashboard) and the other is server-side rendering (not used). (2) Tailwind 4 upgrade, a large separate migration.

## Risk of leaving it
Low. The remaining findings are not reachable in this app.

## When to revisit
If the app starts navigating to user-supplied URLs, uses server rendering, or when URL-per-screen (UXD-002 / D5) is done, because that touches the router anyway.

## What to do then
Do the router v7 upgrade together with UXD-002. Plan Tailwind 4 as its own release with screenshot checks.

## Needed from you
None now.
