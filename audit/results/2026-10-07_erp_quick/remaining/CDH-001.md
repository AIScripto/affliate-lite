# CDH-001: Five files hold about 6,800 lines of tangled logic

**Severity:** Medium  
**Status:** Left on purpose

## Why it was left
Splitting these files means moving logic that sits next to the scoring and report code. A mistake could change a score, and the owner rule is that tested scoring is never touched.

## Risk of leaving it
None for users. It only makes future changes slower and riskier.

## When to revisit
Large new features planned in those files, or a clear need to split them.

## What to do then
Split into per-feature modules in small steps, scoring tests unchanged and run after every step. Stop and ask if any scoring file would change.

## Needed from you
None. It is optional.
