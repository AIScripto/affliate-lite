# SEC-013: Account lockout can be triggered by anyone and re-locks after one wrong try

**Severity:** Medium  
**Status:** Done, extra rules only if needed

## Why it was left
Done and live: 5 wrong passwords lock the account for 15 minutes, a login limit of 10 per minute per address, and an admin Unlock button (ADMIN only, audited as UNLOCK_USER). Left: stricter rules (for example slowing down by address, or alerts) because nothing shows guessing attacks today and extra rules also annoy real users.

## Risk of leaving it
Low. A person who is locked out waits 15 minutes or asks an admin to unlock.

## When to revisit
If the logs show many failed logins from one address, or for many users.

## What to do then
Add an address-based slowdown or an alert, with tests.

## Needed from you
None.
