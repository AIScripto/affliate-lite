# OPS-005: Images are published under a personal Docker Hub account

**Severity:** Medium  
**Status:** Waiting for your decision (D2)

## Why it was left
Moving to a company registry changes the pipeline, the deploy script and the VM login, and it needs a registry that the company owns. That is your decision, not a code fix.

## Risk of leaving it
Low today. Deploys work. The risk is one personal account holding the production images.

## When to revisit
When the company has a registry, or the account owner changes.

## What to do then
Create the registry, change the pipeline push, the compose image names and `deploy.sh`, then deploy once and keep the old images for rollback.
