SEC-002Rate limiting missing on public authentication endpoints
MMediumU1 This sprintSecurityerp-platformconfirmedEffort S (~3h)Fix risk lowfixed#2
Evidence

In backend/src/app.ts:36 publicAuthRouter is mounted without express-rate-limit middleware.

backend/src/app.ts:36 backend/src/routes/auth.routes.ts:15

Fix

Add express-rate-limit or Redis token bucket to publicAuthRouter with max 5 attempts per minute per IP.

Proof test: Integration test sending 6 rapid login requests verifies 429 Too Many Requests response.

Why it matters

Allows attackers to run brute-force dictionary attacks against user passwords.

Appendix: how to read this report

Severity (how bad)

  • CCritical Direct path to serious breach, data loss or outage with little precondition.
  • HHigh Likely significant incident or a broken core guarantee, with some precondition.
  • MMedium Real weakness with limited reach or unusual conditions.
  • LLow Hardening, polish or small maintainability issue.

Urgency (how soon)

  • U0 Now
  • U1 This sprint
  • U2 Next release
  • U3 Planned
  • U4 Backlog

U0 within 24 to 72 hours · U1 this sprint · U2 next 1 to 2 releases · U3 this quarter · U4 backlog.

Sequence is the recommended order to fix things. It follows urgency first, then dependencies (blocked-by), then quick wins, and puts risky changes last. It is a recommendation, not an instruction.

Confidence: confirmed (traced or reproduced) · likely (strong evidence, not fully traced) · suspected (worth a human look).

Quick win: high impact, small effort, low fix risk.
